Ecommerce personalization is customizing the online shopping experience for individual customers. Instead of showing shoppers the same generic messages and products, you tailor marketing messages, product recommendations, and offers to align with their unique preferences. Ecommerce personalization tools track customer preferences, purchase history, and shopping habits.
